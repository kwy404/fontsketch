fontsketch

A free, open source font maker. Draw, import or scan your handwriting and export real TTF, OTF, WOFF and WOFF2 fonts, all in your browser.

Made by Alexandre Kohler (GitHub and LinkedIn).

Built with Monsoon. Interactive parts built with Voodoo.js.
